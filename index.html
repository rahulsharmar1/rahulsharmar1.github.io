About

I'm a backend software engineer with 2+ years of hands-on experience designing and deploying secure, scalable REST APIs and Conversational AI systems using Python, Django, and Django REST Framework. My path into engineering started with a curiosity about how systems work — and how they break. Early work in Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing shaped a security-first mindset that still guides how I design software.

At CognoAI I delivered 50+ production deployments of Conversational AI bots across Web, Android, and WhatsApp — building backend logic and REST API integrations for startups, Indian financial institutions, and enterprise clients across MENA and Saudi Arabia. I applied OWASP-aligned security controls and Docker-based containerization throughout the development lifecycle, and validated APIs with pytest before release.

I enjoy work where backend engineering directly improves reliability and business outcomes: hardening APIs, containerizing services, debugging production issues to root cause, and shipping systems that are functional, resilient, and repeatable to deploy.
